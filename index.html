About

About Essence of Childcare University

Created to give families dependable child care in an environment that combines safety, learning and fun.

We believe children learn best when they feel secure, supported and valued. Our team focuses on building positive relationships with children and families while providing consistent routines, age-appropriate activities and opportunities for children to develop socially, emotionally, physically and academically.

ECU is a licensed childcare center serving children from 4 weeks through 7 years old, with a licensed capacity of 30 children. We are at 5644 W. Appleton Ave in Milwaukee, open Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 12:00 AM.

Our mission. To provide a safe, nurturing and educational child care environment where children can learn through play, build confidence, develop healthy relationships and prepare for their next stage of growth.

Enrollment

Enrollment at ECU

We are excited that you are considering ECU for your child. Enrollment is based on classroom availability and completion of all required forms and records.

Six steps

How to enroll

  1. Contact ECUCall or message us to ask about availability in your child's age group.
  2. Schedule a tourVisit the center, meet staff and have an enrollment conversation.
  3. Complete the applicationFill out the enrollment application and all required forms.
  4. Provide recordsSubmit health and immunization records plus emergency contact information.
  5. Set up paymentComplete payment arrangements, including the registration fee when applicable.
  6. Get your start dateReceive your confirmed start date and classroom information.

What to have ready

  • Child's full legal name and date of birth
  • Parent or guardian contact information
  • Emergency contacts and authorized pickup persons
  • Health and immunization information
  • Allergy, medication or special care information
  • Wisconsin Shares authorization, if applicable
  • Completed forms and signed center agreements

Parent FAQ

Questions families ask

What ages do you accept?

ECU serves children from 4 weeks through 7 years old, subject to classroom availability.

What are your hours?

ECU is open Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 12:00 AM, except scheduled holidays, closures or announced temporary schedule changes.

Do you accept Wisconsin Shares?

Yes. Families using Wisconsin Shares must complete the required eligibility and authorization process. ECU also accepts private pay.

Are meals and snacks provided?

ECU provides CACFP-approved meals and snacks according to the center's meal plan and program schedule. Tell us about food allergies, dietary restrictions or special feeding instructions.

What should my child bring?

Required items depend on age and classroom. Families receive a classroom-specific supply list before the child's start date. Diapers and wipes are supplied by the center.

Can I tour the center?

Yes. Contact ECU to request a tour and discuss current openings. Tours are scheduled during open hours.

How do you communicate with parents?

ECU communicates with families about attendance, care needs, reminders, incidents, classroom updates and other important information through approved center communication methods.

Privacy policy

How Essence of Childcare University handles information collected through this website.

What we collect

This website collects only the information you type into a form: your name, phone number, email address, your child's name and age, the schedule you need, and any message you send. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

Form submissions are used to answer your question, check classroom availability, schedule a tour or process an employment inquiry. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

Children's information

Child and family records held by the center are confidential and kept according to center policy and Wisconsin child care licensing requirements. No private child or family information is published on this website. Photographs of children appear only where ECU holds the appropriate parent or guardian permission for website and social media use.

Withdrawing permission

You can ask us to delete an inquiry you submitted, or to remove a photograph of your child, by calling or texting 1 (414) 791-8637.
